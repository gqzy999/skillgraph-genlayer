      How this works, and its limits

      Endorsement weight is whole points only: ENDORSED +2, WEAK +1, REJECTED +0. A claim can be endorsed once; a second attempt fails. Each account may hold at most 32 claims, and may claim the same skill only once (case-insensitive). The verdict judges the fetched evidence page against the claimed skill only; it does not verify identity, employment, or off-chain work. Evidence pages can change after endorsement; the weight stays as agreed. No funds move in this contract.

      SkillGraph runs on Stable Studionet (chain 61999). Skills and claims are read from the chain; nothing here is demo data.
